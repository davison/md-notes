---
title: Keeping a commonplace book
tags: [reading]
---

People kept commonplace books for centuries: one notebook, everything worth
remembering copied into it, with an index at the back. The index was the
clever part. Search is the same idea without the copying out by hand.

> Write it down while you still believe you will remember it without doing so.

What it suggests for notes that last:

- One place, not five.
- Your own words beside the quotation, or you will not know why you kept it.
- Plain text outlives every application that has ever opened it.
