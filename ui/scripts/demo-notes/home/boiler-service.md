title: Boiler service
tags: [home]
---

Serviced every October. The engineer's number is on the invoice in the
filing box, not here.

- Pressure should sit between 1.0 and 1.5 bar when cold.
- Bleed the upstairs radiators first, then top the pressure back up.
- The condensate pipe freezes below about −5 °C: warm water, not boiling.
